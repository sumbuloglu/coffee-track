 THE BARISTA'S HANDBOOK

A recipe for success.

Select a customer card. Their order shows below their name. Tap matching menu items to fill your tray, then tap Serve.

Each shift lasts 60 seconds. Customers leave when their patience runs out. Orders must match exactly, including quantities.

+100 points for each correct order, plus a combo bonus of 25 per previous consecutive order (maximum +100). Earn the menu price plus up to $6 in speed tips. A wrong tray loses 20 points and resets your combo. A missed customer loses 30 points and resets your combo. Scores never fall below zero.

Buy upgrades during the break between days. The machine raises item revenue, cozy decor increases patience, and a bigger counter adds a customer slot.
